How to play

  1. Press play. Tap the big round button. Sound starts after your first tap.
  2. Tap the squares. Each row belongs to one mochi. Tap a square to place a sound. On Bell Bun and Pudding Pluck, tap it again to change the note. On Your Sound, the number on a square is the part of your song it plays.
  3. Pick a flavor. Strawberry, Matcha, Taro and Mango change the tempo, swing and musical scale.
  4. Hit Remix! Get a fresh beat in that flavor. Not happy? Tap Remix again, or Clear all squares to start empty.
  5. Shape each mochi. Use Loud, Pitch, Echo and Muffle on every row. Press a mochi's mute button and it falls asleep.
  6. Add your own sound. On Your Sound, press Swap in your sound and pick a song or drop a file. Drag the glowing box on the wave to choose which part of the song to use, and tap a length like 2 s or Whole song. The song plays exactly as it is, lyrics included.
  7. Cut it into parts. With "Parts 1 to 4", the glowing box is split into four colored parts. Press the numbers under "Hear a part" to listen to each one. The number on a blue square is the part it plays, so tap a square again to switch between 1, 2, 3 and 4. A single square repeats the start of its part every loop. Short parts of 1 to 4 s work best for beats. Choose "The whole part" and every square plays the whole thing, with no numbers.
  8. Save it as an MP3. Pick how many loops, press Save remix, and keep the page open while it records. The MP3 downloads to your device.
  9. Share it. Press Share to copy a link that rebuilds your beat, tempo, flavor and sleeping mochi. A sound you added yourself is not in the link.

What is Mochi Mixer?

Mochi Mixer is a free online beat maker that runs right in your browser. Six cute mochi each play a sound: a kick drum, a clap, a shaker, a bell, a plucked string, and any song or sound you add. Tap the squares to place sounds, press play, and hit Remix for a fresh beat.

Can I remix my own song?

Yes. Add an audio file on the blue mochi, pick which part of the song to use, and cut it into four parts that you can place anywhere in your beat. Your file stays on your device.

Can I save and share my beat?

Yes. Save remix records your beat and downloads it as an MP3. Share copies a link that rebuilds your beat, tempo and flavor for anyone who opens it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox on phones, tablets and computers. Sound starts after your first tap.
